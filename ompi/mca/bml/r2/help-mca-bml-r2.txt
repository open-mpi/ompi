[unreachable proc]
At least one pair of MPI processes are unable to reach each other for
MPI communications.  This means that no Open MPI device has indicated
that it can be used to communicate between these processes.  This is
an error; Open MPI requires that all MPI processes be able to reach
each other.  This error can sometimes be the result of forgetting to
specify the "self" BTL.

  Process 1 (%s) is on host: %s
  Process 2 (%s) is on host: %s
  BTLs attempted: %s

Your MPI job is now going to abort; sorry.
#
[peer info unavailable]
One MPI process is unable to reach another, because the run-time system
never delivered the second process' connection information.  No Open MPI
device could be selected without it: rather than every device reporting
that it cannot reach this peer, none of them was ever in a position to
answer.

  Process 1 (%s) is on host: %s
  Process 2 (%s) is on host: %s

This is not a matter of which devices are available, so selecting
different ones will not help.  Re-running the job with "--mca
pmix_base_verbose 1" will report the error the run-time system gave
when the information was requested.

Your MPI job is now going to abort; sorry.
